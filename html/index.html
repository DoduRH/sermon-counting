How to interpret the graphs

Interaction tips: Choose a church and graph type using the radio buttons. Use the embedded plot's year slider to pick a starting year (plots show data from that year to the latest). Keyboard arrow keys can also be used to step the slider.
Bar Chart: Percentage of verses covered for each book in the Bible. The y-axis shows percentage of the book covered across that period.
Count: Raw number of sermons that reference each book, aggregated over the selected period.
Line Chart: X-axis lists verses as "Book Chapter:Verse"; the line shows how many times each verse was visited in total over the selected period.
Stacked Bar Chart: For each book, the lower segment shows verses visited and the upper segment shows unread verses, aggregated over the selected period.
